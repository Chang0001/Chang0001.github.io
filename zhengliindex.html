感谢你使用「疯狂来整理」。我们非常重视你的隐私。本政策说明本 App 如何处理与你有关的信息。

1一、我们不收集任何个人信息

本 App 不需要注册或登录，不收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片、设备标识符或任何可识别个人身份的信息。

2二、App 不联网

本 App 的全部内容（关卡、图片、音乐、音效）都随安装包一起提供，运行过程中不会向任何服务器发送数据，也不会下载任何内容。

3三、仅保存在本机的数据

为了记住你的游戏进度，本 App 会在你的设备本地保存以下内容：

· 已通关的关卡与星级

· 每关的最佳用时

· 音乐 / 音效 / 震动 开关状态

这些数据只存在于你的设备上，不会上传，我们也无法访问。卸载 App 即会一并删除。

4四、没有第三方服务

本 App 不集成任何第三方统计、广告、推送或社交 SDK，不会与任何第三方共享信息。

5五、没有广告与内购

本 App 不含广告，也不含任何应用内购买。

6六、儿童隐私

本 App 适合所有年龄段，不收集任何用户（包括儿童）的个人信息。

7七、政策更新

如果将来本政策发生变化，我们会在 App 内更新本页面，并修改下方的生效日期。

8八、联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请通过「设置 → 用户反馈」联系我们，或发送邮件至 1664660979@qq.com，我们会在 3 个工作日内回复。

Thank you for using Tidy Up Frenzy. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the app handles information related to you.

1We collect no personal information

The app requires no registration or login. We do not collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information.

All content (levels, images, music, sound effects) is bundled with the app. Nothing is sent to any server and nothing is downloaded at runtime.

3Data stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the app stores the following locally:

· Completed levels and earned stars

· Best completion time per level

This data exists only on your device and is never uploaded; we have no access to it. Deleting the app removes it.

4No third-party services

The app integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, push, or social SDKs, and shares no information with any third party.

5No ads, no in-app purchases

The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

6Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from any user, including children.

7Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page inside the app and revise the effective date below.

8Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us via Settings → Feedback in the app, or email us at 1664660979@qq.com. We usually reply within 3 business days.
